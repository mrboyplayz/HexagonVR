HEXAGON VR v0.3.1-beta.1: TWO-HAND AIM, TRACKED ARMS, AND HATS

1. Close the old HexagonVR helper and leave any old test server.
   Every VR player must upgrade. v0.2.1-beta.1 and earlier cannot send grips.
2. Start SteamVR and connect the headset and controllers.
3. Run Start-Studio-Test.cmd to try the included offline place, or start
   HexagonVR.exe before joining a game containing the updated support scripts.
4. The test starts with the menu closed. Y is the only menu shortcut.
   B and X are unassigned. Chat and recentering are inside the menu.

Hold either GRIP to pick up; release to drop.
You can point at a ground pickup within 6 studs and hold grip to bring it
to your hand. You do not need to bend to the floor. Grip keeps trying
when held as you move closer. Nearby pickup surfaces are easier to grab.
Crowbar: full size, pointing forward from the shaft grip; swing to hit.
Hats: touch your hat or somebody else's hat and hold GRIP to remove it.
It pivots around the point where you grabbed it. Release near your head or
another player's head to equip it there. Release elsewhere to drop it on
the ground; you can pick it up again. Everyone sees the same original hat.
Hats require a nearby touch, without distance grab.
Other loose ground hats are deleted unless a VR player handled/dropped them.
Pistol: pick up with one hand, then use the other hand to pick up a magazine.
Keep holding grip. Guide the magazine's top into the bottom opening,
then push it up the well until it clicks. It only locks when fully seated.
Releasing it halfway drops it instead of completing the reload.
Grab the slide, pull back a few centimeters, and release or move it forward.
Use the holding hand's TRIGGER to fire. Release it between shots.
To remove the magazine, grip its bottom and pull down.
After the last shot, insert a magazine and pull/release the slide again.

AK-47: use a curved AK magazine (30 rounds), then grab the charging handle
on the receiver's right side, pull back, and release or return it forward.
Hold trigger for automatic fire. Grip the fore-end with your other hand
to stabilize aiming and reduce recoil. The rear grip stays fixed and the
front grip captures your actual hand placement. The AK bolt does not lock
when empty. AK magazines follow a guided rock-in path as you push them in.
The foregrip responds locally on squeeze. Both hand positions drive aim,
with rear-wrist roll, no old angle limit, and no extra aiming filter.
Your own arms are 45% transparent and track independently of body physics.

Both guns have immediate firing sound, spring recoil, muzzle flash,
controller vibration, textured cartridge cases, and confirmed impact effects.
The offline demo includes two practice targets that respawn after being shot.

RIGHT STICK: hold left/right for smooth turning.
Y -> Settings -> Turn Speed: use minus/plus to adjust from 15 to 180 degrees/s.
Default is 65. Changes apply immediately and are saved for the next launch.
Partial stick tilt turns more slowly.
Held magazines and two-hand guns use stable local visuals, with small tracking
movements filtered so server position updates cannot tug them between frames.
The torso follows your head turn partially and stays connected at the neck.
The camera sits at your eyes in front of the avatar head. Look down to see
your body. Turns pivot around the eyes rather than orbiting around the root.

The magazine holds 12 rounds. Inserting a magazine alone does not chamber one.
Removing it does not remove a round already in the chamber.
Opening a VR menu drops held items and blocks weapon actions.

The support scripts are published to Hexagon VR Test (place 105279),
version 15, with Filtering Disabled. Join a fresh server for the new scripts.
The published file's SHA-256 was verified. See PUBLISH-RECEIPT.json.
The public download includes the separate offline VR-Demo.rbxlx.
Each VR player needs this EXE.
PC players can see and hear weapons without installing the helper or assets.
The live place uses verified public Roblox meshes, textures, and recordings.
The offline demo still uses the bundled files installed by the helper.
